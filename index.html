About Research Teaching CV

I am Associate Professor of Economics and Quantitative Analysis at Philips University, Nicosia. I was previously Senior Lecturer and Lecturer of Economics at Cardiff University and Senior Researcher at the Central Bank of Cyprus, and I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Virginia.

My research studies business cycles, labour markets and economic growth, and more recently the macroeconomics of cryptocurrencies. It has been published in journals including the Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control, the Canadian Journal of Economics and Macroeconomic Dynamics.
